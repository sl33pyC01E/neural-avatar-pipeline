Quality and runtime








Faster quantizes only the language model; flow and audio decoding stay at full precision with 10 steps by default. Compare its sound with the approved full-precision baseline. All INT8 and fewer flow steps may trade quality for speed. Settings save automatically.


Prepared clips keep pause shortening. Raw streaming has untrimmed pauses and can stall between phrases. Changing models or threads reloads Pocket; switching tabs keeps used speech and face engines warm while memory allows.
